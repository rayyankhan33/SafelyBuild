# Risk register (project risks, not construction risks)

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Team cannot explain AI-drafted code at the evaluation | Medium | High (marks, Form 11) | Walkthrough guide in implementation.md; each member runs the tests and traces one frame from camera to dashboard; change something small live | All |
| R2 | **Vision accuracy on real footage is unknown** (the one pretrained model tried, YOLO-World zero-shot, found nothing useful on the sample photos). Classical methods are validated only on synthetic frames; false alarms or large stock errors could trigger wrong alerts and erode investor trust | High | High | Say so in UI and docs; engineer view shows the model used; `evaluate.py` and the training-set export exist; Sprint 2 priority is real-footage evaluation; a trained model plugs in without code changes. **Update:** a trained crack model now exists as an opt-in plugin and it too over-fires on stained/textured concrete ([crack-model.md](crack-model.md) 4.2), so it is not active by default; crack severity is capped at High | Hadi + teammate |
| R3 | ACI table values or clause numbers transcribed incorrectly | Medium | High (wrong budgets) | Verify each table against a licensed copy; estimation tests use hand calculations | Rayyan |
| R4 | Default prices and productivity are placeholders; investors may read the budget as real | High | Medium | Labelled demo values; admin-only price book; each estimate stores the prices it used | Rayyan |
| R5 | Forecasts are noisy early in a project | Medium | Medium | No forecast under 3 logged days; range shown; documented thresholds | Rayyan |
| R6 | **A camera can be obstructed, redirected or powered off** to hide problems (silence is detected, obstruction is not) | Medium | High | Overdue and uncovered alerts exist; Sprint 2: frame-change and scene-consistency checks, camera health pings | Rayyan |
| R7 | Image-based stock and progress are noisy or biased on real sites; the 2 % stock deadband and the stock constants (angle of repose, packing densities, ground scale) are calibrated on synthetic data | High | High | Documented constants; calibrate on real frames in Sprint 2; consistent bias matters less than noise (documented for model authors) | Hadi |
| R8 | Single point of knowledge: one member did most commits | Medium | Medium | Each member commits their own area; milestone log | All |
| R9 | Frames may show workers; monitoring and privacy obligations | Medium now, High at launch | High | Consent, notice, retention and access policy before any pilot; measure materials and structures, not people | Team |
| R10 | Docker deployment untested | Medium | Low | Test compose on a machine with Docker | Atyab |
| R11 | An administrator is trusted: an admin (or anyone with database access) could change data or install a camera that feeds false frames | Low | High | Restrict database access in deployment; ship the audit log to a separate system; camera installs are audited; consider two-person approval for new cameras | Rayyan |
| R12 | Two people train and integrate models at once; the stage vocabulary or output format may drift from what the platform expects | Medium | Medium | Single contract in model-integration.md; registry validates plugins; `test_registry.py`; agree the stage vocabulary early | Hadi + teammate |
| R13 | The synthetic demo may be mistaken for real results | Medium | High | `is_demo` flag, banners in both UIs, disclosure in tool-disclosure.md, fidelity wording says "synthetic" everywhere | All |
